Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Budget Planner has no server, no accounts, and no analytics. The figures you type are never transmitted anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account to create, no form that submits anywhere, and no tracking or analytics of any kind — no cookies, no pixels, no third-party scripts.

How the calculations work

The calculation engine is compiled from Rust to WebAssembly and runs inside your browser. Transactions, category budgets, goals, debts and any CSV you import and every other figure you enter are processed on your own device and are never sent over the network.

Your budget data

Everything you enter writes to your browser's IndexedDB storage — local to that browser on that device. It is not synced, backed up, or transmitted. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it permanently, and we have no copy.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub's infrastructure handles the network requests needed to deliver the page and may log technical details such as IP addresses. That is standard web hosting and is outside our control; it is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. We receive no data from it and have no access to those logs.

Beyond loading the page itself, the app sends nothing out — no figure you type and no receipt ever leaves your device. It does make one kind of outgoing request, described next, and it only ever downloads. You can verify this in your browser's developer tools: open the Network tab and use the app — nothing is sent.

Third-party models

Receipt scanning and statement import each run a machine-learning model in your browser. Those model files are not part of the initial page load: the first time you use one of those features, the app downloads the model it needs from this same site and then runs it entirely on your device. The download is one-way — it fetches the model file and sends nothing back. If you never open those features, nothing is ever downloaded.

ModelUsed forLicense
PP-OCRv6_tiny (detection + recognition) Reading text off a photographed receipt Apache-2.0
all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Deciding whether an unsigned statement row is income or expense Apache-2.0

Both run in pure Rust in your browser via rten. The two PP-OCRv6_tiny files were converted to that runtime's own .rten file format first; all-MiniLM-L6-v2 ships as the ONNX file it was downloaded as. That conversion changes the file format only, not the model's architecture or its trained weights. Full attribution, including the required notices and the statement of what was changed, is in MODEL-LICENSES.md.

Full license text for every open-source component this app is built from, including these models and every Rust crate compiled into it, is on the open source licenses page.

Children

The app is a general-purpose budgeting tool and is not directed at children. Since no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be published here with a new date above. Because the app collects nothing, any change that introduced data collection would be a significant one and called out in the app itself.
